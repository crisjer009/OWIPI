Store count supervision and locator management
🎯 Key Purpose at a Glance
The physical counting workstation for supervisors.
Allows supervisors to monitor all wireless scanners in real-time, audit individual shelves, print count summaries, export Excel variance reports, and push final counts to cloud.
⚡ Available Actions / Controls
Monitor Scanners: View live barcode streams.
Close Locators: Lock shelf counts to prevent tampering.
Export & Sync: Generate Excel files and push to Cloud.
💡 Pro-Tip & Best Practice
Keep the Host Console open on your supervisor laptop while floor staff are counting shelves with handheld devices.
